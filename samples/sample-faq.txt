ShopEase Returns Policy

You can return most products within 14 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. To start a return, open your order page and click "Request return".

Refunds are sent to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the product. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your bank account or wallet.

Items that cannot be returned: underwear, opened cosmetics, digital gift cards, and products marked as final sale.

ShopEase Shipping

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days inside Pakistan. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days and costs Rs. 350 extra.

Orders above Rs. 5,000 get free standard shipping. We ship to all major cities. Remote areas may take 2 more days.

You will get a tracking number by SMS and email once your order is shipped.

ShopEase Payments

We accept debit and credit cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery has an extra fee of Rs. 100.

If your payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed, do not pay again. The amount is automatically returned within 3 working days. You can also contact support with your transaction ID.

ShopEase Support

Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm. You can email support@shopease.example or use the chat button on the website. We reply within 24 hours.
